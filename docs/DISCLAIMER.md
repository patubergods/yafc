The rear camera is a safety aid. A changed picture can mislead you. **Do not rely on YAFC to
decide whether it is safe to move.** Look around the vehicle yourself, always.

Treat YAFC as an experiment for **off-road use, a private closed area, or a parked vehicle only**.
Do not use it on a public road, and do not use it in any situation where a wrong or missing camera
picture could hurt someone. Install, test and remove it only with the vehicle **stationary, the brake
held** and no one behind it. If you are not sure you can accept that risk, do not install it.
